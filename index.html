FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Ask me directly.

How long does a typical project take?

Two to six weeks depending on scope. Marketing sites land at the short end; dashboards and apps take longer.

Do you work with existing code?

Yes. I will audit the codebase first and tell you honestly whether a rebuild or an incremental fix is better value.

What does it cost?

Landing pages start at $1,500. Larger sites are quoted after the first call. Fixed price, never hourly surprises.

Can I update the site myself afterwards?

Yes. You get a static CMS or clear documentation so you can change text, images and pricing without a developer.
